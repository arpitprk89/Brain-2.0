Tips library

Practical, not preachy. Open whichever matches how you feel right now.

My workload feels too big to even start High load

Pick the single most urgent task and give it 25 minutes, nothing else. Write everything else on a list and close it — an unfinished list in your head costs more energy than one on paper.

I studied for hours but retained nothing Any

Recall beats re-reading. Close the book and try to write out what you remember from memory before checking — it feels harder but sticks far longer.

I keep sleeping late "to catch up"

Late-night study past a point has negative returns — tired recall is unreliable. Protecting even 7 hours usually outperforms an extra 2 hours of exhausted study.

Exam pressure is making me panic

Break the exam into topics and rank them by how shaky you feel, not by syllabus order. Studying your weakest topic first, even briefly, reduces anxiety faster than reviewing what you already know.

I feel like I have no one to talk to about this

A short message to one trusted person — a teacher, parent, or friend — counts as support. If it ever feels unmanageable, that's worth a real conversation with an adult you trust, not just this tool.

Everything feels fine Low load

Good day to build a buffer — get ahead on a topic you'll need later, or genuinely rest. Both count as progress.

How the belief update works

Each check-in is scored on five weighted signals into a single load estimate between 0 and 1. That estimate is compared against three reference profiles (Low ≈ 0.25, Moderate ≈ 0.5, High ≈ 0.75) using a Gaussian likelihood, then combined with your prior belief — carried over from your last check-in — using Bayes' rule, and renormalised into the posterior shown above. Run it a few days in a row and you're watching your own belief state evolve with the evidence.

This is a self-reflection tool, not a clinical or diagnostic instrument. If workload or stress feels unmanageable, talking to a teacher, family member, or counsellor is worth more than any score on this page.
